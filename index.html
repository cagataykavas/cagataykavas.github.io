M.SC. RESEARCH · 2026

Struct-XAI

Compact, inspectable interpretability for instruction-tuned LLMs.

My thesis work investigates hidden states, attribution, layer-wise behavior and activation patching in Turkish language models. The recorded evaluation includes 31 model-prompt cases: 14 for each of two Qwen models and three for Gemma, across five task categories.

The research evaluation uses compact instruction-model tasks to expose model-specific blind spots, decision instability and scaling behavior while keeping methodological limits explicit.
